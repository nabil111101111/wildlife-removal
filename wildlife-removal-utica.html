The animals Utica homeowners call about most

Squirrels in attics before winter

Utica's gray squirrels start claiming attic space in fall. They chew in through fascia and gable vents, stockpile nuts in insulation, and gnaw wiring : a fire risk in homes running furnaces hard all winter.

Raccoons in chimneys

The many uncapped brick chimneys on Utica's older homes are classic raccoon dens. A chimney raccoon in spring usually means kits, soot tracked through the flue, and a blockage risk for furnace exhaust.

Bats in older attics

Utica's pre-war homes have the ridge gaps and loose flashing bats need. Colonies hibernate in these attics, and homeowners often discover them via a stray bat in the house mid-winter.

Skunks under porches and garages

Skunks den under Utica porches, garages and sheds, digging in before the ground freezes. Spring litters under a porch mean months of odor risk.

Signs an animal is already inside

  • Droppings below a roofline gap or in one attic corner
  • Rub marks beside a hole or vent
  • Flattened insulation with nesting material in piles
  • Chewed fascia, soffit corners or gable vent screens
  • Urine or musk smell on warm afternoons
  • Daylight visible through the roofline from the attic

The four step process, in the right order

Order matters. Seal too early and an animal can be trapped in a wall. Skip the seal and the next animal uses the same hole.

1. Inspection

Attic, roofline and foundation checked, entry points photographed. Written scope and price first.

2. Humane removal

One-way doors let animals leave and not return; ground animals are live caught. No poison.

3. Exclusion and sealing

Every gap closed with chew-proof metal, mesh or flashing. Chimneys capped, vents screened.

4. Cleanup

Droppings come out, the space disinfected, damaged insulation flagged.

Do you use poison for animals in the attic in Utica?

No poison is used for attic wildlife in Utica. Poisoned animals die in wall voids, where they smell for weeks and draw flies. Will you seal the entry points in my Utica home, or just remove the animal?

Entry points are sealed on every job in Utica, because removal alone does not last. The animal used a gap at the roofline, a vent or the foundation, and the next animal will find it. Sealing with chew-proof materials ends the cycle. Ask for the seal list in writing before work starts.

New York seasons, blackout dates, and timing in Utica

Can bats be removed from a Utica home during maternity season?

Bats cannot be excluded in Utica during the New York blackout windows, May 1 to August 15 and October 1 to March 31. Flightless pups cannot leave, and sealing them in is cruel and against state guidance. Inspections still run, and exclusion is booked for the day the window opens.

Prevention that holds in Oneida County

  • Fit a stainless chimney cap
  • Screen vents with quarter inch hardware cloth
  • Replace soft fascia and rotted trim
  • Screen under decks and porches with buried mesh
  • Cut branches back six to eight feet from the roofline
  • Store pet food and bird seed in sealed metal bins
