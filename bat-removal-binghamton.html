How bat removal works in Binghamton

  1. Inspect the roofline and attic at the right hour. Bat work starts with a dusk watch or a careful roofline survey to find exit gaps, guano and roost points.
  2. Fit one-way valves at the active exits. In an open work window, one-way valves or tubes go over the active exits.
  3. Seal the whole structure, not just the hole. Every remaining gap is closed with the right material for the spot: metal flashing, backer rod and sealant, vent screening and chimney caps.
  4. Clean up guano and check the attic. After the colony is confirmed out, guano is removed with respirator protection, contaminated insulation is taken out where needed, and the attic is disinfected.

New York protects roosting bats with two blackout windows when exclusion and disturbance are off the table: May 1 to August 15, covering the maternity season when pups cannot fly, and October 1 to March 31, covering winter hibernation for roosting colonies.
