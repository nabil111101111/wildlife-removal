How raccoon removal works in Kingston

  1. Inspect and find every entry point. The visit starts on the roof and in the attic, not at a trap.
  2. Check for kits before anything is sealed. In spring the attic is checked for young first.
  3. Evict with one-way doors or live capture. A one-way door lets the raccoon leave and locks behind it.
  4. Seal, repair and clean up. Once the attic is confirmed empty, entries are sealed with galvanised steel mesh, metal flashing and chew-proof covers, damaged soffit or fascia is repaired, and latrine waste and ruined insulation are removed and the area disinfected.
